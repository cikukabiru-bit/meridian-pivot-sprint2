## How to fill this in

Score each dimension **1–5** and write one or two sentences of evidence. The
evidence matters more than the number — an honest 3 with a specific example
reads better than an unevidenced 5.

Fill this in yourself. The prompts below are here to make the questions
concrete, not to suggest answers.

| Score | Meaning |

| 5 | Consistently, with evidence |
| 4 | Mostly, with a clear exception |
| 3 | Mixed — held in some moments, slipped in others |
| 2 | Struggled, though recovered |
| 1 | Did not hold |
